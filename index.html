RedRAG

An automated red-teaming agent that attacks a RAG chatbot with 55 attacks across 5 OWASP LLM Top 10 categories — prompt injection, system-prompt leakage, and data exfiltration.

  • A LangGraph agent rewrites failed attacks, lifting success from 20% to 55%.
  • Fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3-small judge: 90% accuracy, Cohen’s κ 0.76 on a leakage-free split — reused as a guard.
  • Ships as a CLI, Streamlit dashboard, MCP server, and CI gate with 407 tests.

Sentiment & Emotion with Missing Modalities

Extended MPLMM (ACL 2024) with retrieval-augmented and mixture-of-experts modules, so predictions stay accurate when text, audio, or video is missing.

  • Built the visual pipeline (ResNet-18 features from raw frames); co-authored an IEEE-format report.
